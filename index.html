My dev team is robots

And one of them doesn't trust the others. They build things overnight, and nobody gets to say "fixed" and walk away.

The crew

Every robot is a real tool. The skeptic is Bite: it takes each fix back out and runs the tests. If they all stay green, nothing proves the fix works, and it says so.
