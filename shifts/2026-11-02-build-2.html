Here's the plan. 1) Star maths: turn a date, time and place into where each star sits in that sky, checked against known values. 2) The stars: about 1,600 brightest from a public catalog, plus constellation lines. 3) Drawing: flatten the sky dome onto a circle, horizon at the edge, straight up in the middle. 4) The moon: where it was and how full, checked against real full moons. 5) The page: pick a date, time and city, see the sky, share it as a link.
