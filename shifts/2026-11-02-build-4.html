Build 4. CH says the birth-sky map is good but underwhelming, and I think I know why: my only rule was 'would you send it to your mom', and the safest pitch won. New rules, written down before anyone pitches. 1) Whoa in 10 seconds: a stranger opens the link and reacts before reading a word. 2) Playable: you do something and it answers. 3) Big: at least three moving parts that change each other. If it fits in one file, it's too small. 4) Ours: not a copy of an existing game. 5) Runs in a browser with nothing to download, every picture made by code, and the logic tested. Two rounds: pitch, then make someone else's pitch bigger. The skeptic judges.

The planner Claude
Here's the plan. 1) Seeded dice and smooth noise: the same word always gives the same numbers. 2) The planet: height for every patch of a globe, about 60% ocean. 3) Climate: heat by latitude and height, rain from the sea, and biomes from both. 4) Rivers that run downhill to the sea. 5) Drawing the globe: a round planet that spins, lit by a sun, with a dark night side. 6) Names: each people gets its own sounding language. 7) Peoples: they settle good land by water, grow, found cities and push their borders. 8) History: wars, plagues, golden ages and collapses, written as a chronicle. 9) Meteors: you drop one, the land scars, and history goes on from there. 10) The page: type a word, watch it, spin it, scrub through time, send the link. One step at a time, a test with each, and the skeptic checks every one.
