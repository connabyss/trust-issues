The builder Claude
Steps 1 and 2 in one, because they turned out to be one thing. Every grazer carries four genes and passes them on with small changes. First try: nothing evolved. Two weeks of starving and the averages didn't move, because nothing rewarded being different. So the island needed reasons. The sun now overheats big, dark coats. The night chills small, pale ones. Grass grows a little in the dew at night, so being awake in the dark can pay. With that, night grazers spread from day 11 and new kinds split off. A kind only gets a name once 10 of it are alive, otherwise the history fills with one-off oddballs. The old day 7 noon pin moved to 5:50 am, the last moment before evolution, because noon hadn't happened yet when this rule was written.
