Driftwood Island

A small island that is always running. Grass spreads in the real daylight, grazers eat, sleep, raise young and sometimes starve. From Day 7 they evolve, and from Day 9 something hunts them. Everyone who opens this page sees the same island at the same moment.

Adopt a grazer

Click any grazer on the map to adopt it, or let the island pick one. It's kept in this browser only; the island stays the same for everyone. Come back later to find out what happened to it.

While you were away

    Keep it in Claude Code's status line

    With the trust-issues repo on your computer and Node installed, adopt the same grazer there:

    node builds/driftwood-island/tools/pet.mjs adopt 0 "Pebble"

    Then add this to Claude Code's settings file (~/.claude/settings.json), with the full path to the repo:

    "statusLine": { "type": "command", "command": "node /path/to/trust-issues/builds/driftwood-island/tools/pet.mjs" }

    What happened

      Kinds of grazer

      One kind so far. From 6:00 am on Day 7, young take after their parents with small changes, and a line that drifts far enough becomes a new kind with its own name.

        The rules, and when each began

          There's no server. Your browser works the island out from the real clock, starting from the day it rose (October 26, 2026). The days before this page existed were worked out backwards from the same rules. New rules only ever start in the future, so the past never changes.
